title: Independence of Random Variables
summary: "Two random variables X and Y are independent when their joint distribution factorises as the product of the marginals — F(x, y) = F_X(x) F_Y(y), or equivalently the joint density (or PMF) factorises. This checkpoint gives the definition, proves the factorisation E[XY] = E[X] E[Y] for independent integrable variables, and explains the difference between pairwise and mutual independence for collections of more than two variables."
