title: Conditional Expectation
summary: "The conditional expectation E[X | B] is the expectation of X under the conditional probability P(· | B); more generally, E[X | Y] is the random variable that takes value E[X | Y = y] when Y = y. This checkpoint defines conditional expectation in the discrete and absolutely continuous cases, presents it as a function of the conditioning variable, and develops the linearity, monotonicity, and 'taking out what is known' properties that make it the natural projection onto the σ-algebra generated by Y."
