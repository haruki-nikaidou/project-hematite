This checkpoint develops the definition, distinguishes discrete from absolutely continuous random variables, introduces the cumulative distribution function (CDF), probability mass function (PMF), and probability density function (PDF), and explains why measurability is exactly the condition that makes probabilities of events about the variable well-defined."
